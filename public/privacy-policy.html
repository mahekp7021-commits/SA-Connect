S&A Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

S&A Connect is a Business Communication & CRM platform. This policy explains how information submitted through connected landing pages and CRM workflows may be processed.

Information

Depending on client configuration, the service may process name, phone number, email address, location details, enquiry details and business communication metadata.

Use

Information is used to route enquiries to the relevant client, provide CRM features, maintain security and operate requested communication services.

Security

Credentials and service tokens are intended to remain server-side and are not exposed in public landing-page code.

Contact

For questions about a specific enquiry, contact the business that operates the relevant S&A Connect landing page.
